# Harbor Coffee Roasters — brewing guide

A file to upload during the demo, so the "add a source and watch it index" step has
something real to index. It is deliberately outside the seeded knowledge base:
ask the bot about brew ratios before uploading it and it will say it does not know.

## Filter (V60)

15g of coffee to 250g of water at 94°C. Rinse the paper, bloom with 50g of water for
40 seconds, then pour in two stages. Total brew time 2:45.

## Espresso

18g in, 36g out, 27 to 31 seconds. Grind finer if the shot runs fast, coarser if it
chokes. Our Deep roast wants the shorter end of that range.

## Cafetière

60g of coffee per litre, ground coarse. Four minutes, then break the crust and skim
before plunging.

## Cold brew

100g of coarse coffee per litre of cold water. Steep for 16 hours at room temperature
or 24 hours in the fridge, then filter through a paper cone. Keeps for five days.

## Water

Coffee is 98% water, so use filtered water at roughly 150ppm total hardness. Distilled
water extracts badly and tastes flat.
